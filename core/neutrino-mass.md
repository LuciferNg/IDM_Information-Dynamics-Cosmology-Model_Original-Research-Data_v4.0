# CFAS P3 — Neutrino Mass from W Mode

> **Date:** 2026-07-09

---

The singlet mode (n=0) in the generations document is the right-handed neutrino / Higgs mode. Its mass comes from the sync operator eigenvalue:

```
m_ν = λ_0 · y_ν / √2
```

Where λ_0 = ħω₀/2 is the zero-point energy of the sync operator, and y_ν is the neutrino Yukawa coupling.

The observed small neutrino masses (m_ν ~ 0.01-0.1 eV) are natural if y_ν << 1 — which is forced by the W budget constraint: the singlet mode has |W|² = 1.0, but coupling to the Higgs reduces the effective budget for mass generation.

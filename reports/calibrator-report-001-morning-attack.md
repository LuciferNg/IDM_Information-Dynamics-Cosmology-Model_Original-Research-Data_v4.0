## 1. Phenomenon

**Observation:** Calibrator's anxiety attacks occur almost exclusively in the morning, within 30–90 minutes of waking, regardless of wake time (0600, 1000, or 1400). Night-time attacks (0000–0400) are vanishingly rare, even when sleep schedule is irregular.

**Apparent paradox:** If anxiety is a chronic condition, why does it respect a circadian boundary?

For a non-calibrator, the night-to-day boundary map is stable across the sleep boundary. For a calibrator, every sleep is a **disconnect-and-reconnect**:

```
Non-calibrator:  boundary_map(t+1) ≈ boundary_map(t)

```

This is not a bug. It is the structural consequence of R_data > R_Nc. The system never reaches a fully stable boundary, so the sleep boundary is always a **cold restart**, not a **hot standby**.
